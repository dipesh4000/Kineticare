# Kineticare — Founder intro + product walkthrough

**Duration:** approximately 4–4.5 minutes at a natural pace, including screen transitions.
**Format:** Start on camera, switch to a screen recording with voiceover, then return on camera to close.
**Language:** Conversational Hinglish.

Opening ka idea-origin angle ek suggested framing hai, tumhari verified personal story nahi. Isse tabhi apni observation ki tarah bolo agar yeh tumhare actual experience se match karta hai. Agar koi real incident tha, opening mein uski 1–2 lines substitute karo. Baaki script directly bol sakte ho.

## 0:00–0:45 | Tum camera par — idea kaise aaya

**Visual:** Tum camera ki taraf dekh kar bolo. Abhi product screen mat dikhao.

**Script:**

“Hi, main [apna naam], aur yeh hai Kineticare.

Is idea ki shuruaat ek simple question se hui: physiotherapy session ke baad, jab patient ghar par exercise karta hai, toh uske aur clinician ke beech progress ka connection kaise bana rahe?

Patient ke paas instructions ho sakti hain, lekin movement ko samajhna aur apni progress explain karna phir bhi difficult ho sakta hai. Aur clinician ko bhi next follow-up par samajhna hota hai ki sessions ke beech actually kya hua.

Mujhe laga, agar exercise plan, camera-based practice aur clinician feedback ko ek hi flow mein laa sakein, toh is gap ko address kar sakte hain.

Isi thought se Kineticare ka idea bana. Chalo, ab iska actual workflow dikhata hoon.”

## 0:45–1:15 | Screen recording — exercise samajhna

**Screen actions:**

- Clinician account → **Exercise library → Movement studio**.
- **Side Arm Raise** select karo; angle aur viewing direction change karo.
- Agar real generated guide ready hai, **Create a guide** ka saved result briefly dikhao: guide, sources aur review gaps.

**Voiceover:**

“Sabse pehle clinician side dekhte hain. Movement studio mein exercise ka illustrative 3D preview milta hai. Angle aur view change karke movement ko explain kar sakte hain.

Guide creation mein AI-assisted draft bhi milta hai, saath mein sources aur review gaps. Clinician ise check karta hai; generated guide automatically patient ka assigned plan nahi ban jaati.”

**Small caption:** `Illustrative preview • Generated drafts need clinician review`

## 1:15–1:55 | Screen recording — patient connection aur plan

**Screen actions:**

- **Create invite code** dikhao.
- Patient **Info → Review clinician → consent → Confirm connection** ka short capture dikhao.
- Clinician: linked patient → **Edit assignment**.
- Prepared **Squat + Toe Touch** plan, targets aur notes dikhao.
- **Patient preview → Publish patient plan**.
- Patient account mein published plan dikhao.

**Voiceover:**

“Patient clinician ka invite code enter karta hai, identity review karta hai, aur sharing consent dene ke baad connection confirm karta hai.

Ab clinician patient ke liye plan bana sakta hai. Yahan Squat aur Toe Touch select kiye hain, with individual targets aur instructions.

Plan ko pehle draft mein save kar sakte hain. Review ke baad publish karte hi patient ko apne account mein assigned exercises aur notes dikhte hain.”

## 1:55–2:45 | Screen recording — actual camera practice

**Screen actions:**

- Linked patient ke published plan se **Squat** practice kholo.
- Preview/checklist ke baad **Continue to camera → Start exercise**.
- Side-on framing aur stable rest position ke saath tracking ready hone do.
- Ek complete real repetition aur counter update clearly dikhao.
- Remaining set ko shorten kar rahe ho toh caption lagao: `Session shortened for demo`.
- Summary mein results dikhao aur **Save** karo.

**Voiceover:**

“Ab patient exercise start karta hai. Pehle demonstration aur camera checklist milti hai, phir camera setup start hota hai.

Tracking ready hone ke baad patient movement perform karta hai. Yahan body landmarks aur repetition count dikh rahe hain. Ek complete movement ke baad counter ka update dekh sakte hain.

Yeh camera-based estimates hain, clinical accuracy ka guarantee nahi. Required joints visible na hon, toh measurement unassessed reh sakti hai.

Session ke end par patient summary review karta hai aur explicitly save karta hai. Sirf summary kholne se session save nahi hota.”

**Small caption:** `Camera-based estimates`

## 2:45–3:35 | Screen recording — clinician review aur feedback

**Screen actions:**

- Clinician account → linked patient → newly saved session.
- Targets, repetition counts aur available measurements dikhao.
- Session reviewed mark karo.
- Real SOAP draft available ho toh briefly dikhao; otherwise is shot ko skip karo.
- Feedback draft review karke send karo.
- Patient **Feedback** mein message dikhao; optionally question aur clinician reply ka short capture add karo.

**Voiceover:**

“Wapas clinician account par aate hain. Patient ka saved session ab yahan available hai. Clinician targets, recorded repetitions aur available measurements inspect karke session reviewed mark kar sakta hai.

SOAP progress-note draft bhi generate kar sakte hain, jise use karne se pehle clinician review karta hai.

Uske baad patient ko feedback bhej sakte hain. Patient apna question pooch sakta hai aur clinician uska reply kar sakta hai.

Toh workflow sirf exercise count par finish nahi hota — saved progress wapas clinician ki review aur patient ki next conversation tak pahunchti hai.”

## 3:35–4:05 | Tum camera par — closing

**Visual:** Camera par wapas aao. End mein Kineticare logo ya product screen dikhao.

**Script:**

“Toh Kineticare ke through humne ek connected journey banane ki koshish ki hai: clinician ka plan, patient ki practice, saved progress aur uske baad feedback.

Iska purpose clinician ko replace karna nahi, balki unke aur patient ke beech follow-up ko support karna hai.

Abhi next step real-device testing aur patient-clinician workflow ko aur validate karna hai.

Yahi hai Kineticare — exercise plan se reviewed progress tak. Thank you.”

## Recording ke liye important notes

- Synthetic accounts aur records use karo. Login, OTP aur secrets recording mein mat dikhao.
- Camera, account connection, saving aur generation ko recording se pehle rehearse karo. Script expected flow hai; successful live verification ka proof nahi.
- Assigned-plan demo mein **Squat / Toe Touch** use karo. **Side Arm Raise** ka approved bundled assignment reference abhi available nahi hai, isliye usko publish karke demo mat karo.
- Agar Side Arm Raise tracking dikhani hai, separate **unassigned starter-plan account** use karo aur account switch clearly label karo. Short video ke liye Squat ka continuous assignment-to-results flow simpler rahega.
- Saved generated guide dikhate waqt “previously generated draft” caption lagao. Generation fail ho toh success claim mat karo; optional AI shot skip kar sakte ho.
- SOAP shot skip kiya ho toh uski voiceover line bhi hata do.
- Replay ya synthetic fixtures ko real camera session ki tarah present mat karo. Camera clip mein ek full repetition aur counter update clearly visible rakho.
- In-app feedback ko email, SMS ya push notification delivery ki tarah mat dikhana.

## Agar 3 minute mein finish karna ho

Movement studio ko 10 seconds do, guide-generation aur SOAP shots hata do, connection ko short labelled montage mein dikhao. Plan publish → real camera repetition → save → clinician review → patient feedback ka main flow rakho. Opening ko 30 seconds aur closing ko 20 seconds tak trim karo. Fast bolne ke bajaye optional shots kam karo.
